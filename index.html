What we handle

Everything your building needs, in one place.

One team for the day-to-day, the paperwork and the surprises, so you can stop chasing tenants, contractors and deadlines.

  1. 01

    Leasing and renewals

    We market vacant units, screen applicants, draft leases and send renewal offers on time.

  2. 02

    Rent collection and reporting

    Rent collected and tracked every month, with clean ledgers, rent rolls and owner statements.

  3. 03

    Rent stabilization compliance

    Annual DHCR registrations, renewal notices and the filings that keep stabilized apartments in good standing.

  4. 04

    Maintenance and repairs

    Repair requests answered quickly and handled by trusted contractors from start to finish, with you kept in the loop.

  5. 05

    Violations and inspections

    We watch HPD and DOB records, coordinate inspections and see violations through until they are closed.

  6. 06

    Bills, insurance and books

    Water and utility bills, insurance renewals and certificates, budgets and year-end bookkeeping.
